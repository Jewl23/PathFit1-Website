01 / UNDERSTAND YOUR BODY

More than meets the eye.

Your body is built on a remarkable framework of bones. The skeletal system is made up of bones and cartilage that work together to give your body shape, provide stability, protect delicate organs, and make movement possible. Bones are strong, living connective tissues that constantly support and adapt to the demands placed on your body. From the skull protecting the brain to the rib cage shielding the heart and lungs, every part of the skeleton has an important role. Explore the three essential functions of the skeletal system:

01

PROTECT

Your bones act like a protective shield for some of the body’s most delicate organs. The skull protects the brain, the vertebral column keeps the spinal cord safe, and the rib cage surrounds and protects the heart and lungs.

02

SUPPORTS

Our skeleton provides the framework that keeps our body strong and supported. It gives us our shape, keeps our vital organs in place, and helps us maintain good posture.

03

MOVES

Our bones work together with our muscles to make movement possible. The skeleton provides attachment points for muscles, while joints connect the bones and allow them to move in different ways.

03 / ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

TURNING KNOWLEDGE INTO INTERACTION.

This educational website was created not only as a midterm performance task for Sir Arnel Sabido Jr., but as an opportunity to turn learning into an interactive experience. It was designed to explore the human skeletal system beyond simple names and definitions. Through interactive visuals, a 3D skeletal model, and an engaging quiz, this website aims to make anatomy easier to explore, understand, and remember. It is a small step toward making learning something we can see, interact with, and truly appreciate.
